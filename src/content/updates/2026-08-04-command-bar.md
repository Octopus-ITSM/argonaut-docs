title: Command bar (⌘K)
description: Search and act from anywhere in the back office.
tags:

---

- Press ⌘K (or Ctrl+K) from any page to search across modules: find a record by its number (INC-2026-00021) or by name, reopen recently visited items, and launch creations like "Create an incident" — all without leaving the keyboard.
